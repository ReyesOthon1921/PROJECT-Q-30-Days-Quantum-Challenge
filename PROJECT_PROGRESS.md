# PROJECT-Q 30-Day Quantum Computing Challenge

This repository tracks my PROJECT-Q 30-Day Quantum Computing Challenge work.

## Structure

### Week 1

Days 1–4 were completed as Jupyter notebooks.

- Day 1: Qubits, Quantum States, Hadamard Gate, Superposition, Measurement
- Day 2: Early quantum circuit practice
- Day 3: Continued notebook-based quantum practice
- Day 4: Superposition and Probability Amplitudes

Days 5–6 were completed as Python lab scripts.

- Day 05: Measurement and State Collapse
- Day 06: Single-Qubit Quantum Gates

Day 07 is the Week 1 Mini Project.

- Day 07: Quantum Randomness Laboratory

### Week 2

Week 2 labs are organized as Python scripts.

- Day 08: Understanding Qiskit Architecture
- Day 09: Multi-Qubit Systems
- Day 10: Controlled-NOT / CNOT Gate
- Day 11: Quantum Entanglement
- Day 12: Quantum Teleportation

## Notes

The earlier days use notebooks because they were focused on interactive learning and setup.

The later days use Python scripts because the challenge moved into lab-style coding, CSV outputs, reports, figures, and GitHub commits.

## Repository Goal

The goal of this repository is to document my learning path through quantum computing using Qiskit, including notebooks, lab scripts, outputs, and mini projects.
