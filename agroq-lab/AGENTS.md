# AgroQ Agent Operating Instructions

## Mission

Build a manual-first, offline-capable, scientifically traceable field-research MVP for a one-acre living laboratory.

## Non-negotiable rules

1. Never remove manual entry in favor of automation.
2. No AI or LLM may directly perform a high-impact field action.
3. Every recommendation must show source type, model/rule version, evidence, uncertainty, and approval state.
4. Raw observations are immutable; corrections create a new version or quality flag.
5. Every asset, plot, experiment, model, and action receives a unique ID.
6. The application must still demonstrate its core workflow with no external APIs.
7. Preserve import/export and local backup.
8. Sensor, cloud, AI, optimization, and quantum integrations must use adapters.
9. Label computation truthfully: classical, ML, quantum-inspired, simulator, hardware, or quantum sensor.
10. Add tests before changing safety-sensitive behavior.

## Build order

1. Manual field operations
2. Local database and audit trail
3. Offline queue and synchronization
4. Map and asset registry
5. Experiment and sample tracking
6. Human approval workflow
7. Sensor ingestion adapter
8. Baseline analytics
9. AI model registry
10. Optimization and quantum research adapters
