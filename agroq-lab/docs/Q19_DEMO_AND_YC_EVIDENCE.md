## YC update structure

### Headline

One factual sentence describing the milestone.

### Progress summary

Describe what was built and verified without overstating deployment, users, revenue, hardware, research advantage, or field performance.

### Metrics

Use verifiable metrics such as:

- automated tests passed;
- protected APIs;
- persistent tables;
- completed workflows;
- verified backups;
- staging checks;
- interviews completed;
- pilot candidates;
- evidence files.

### Limitations

State:

- controlled beta versus production;
- simulation versus hardware;
- manual approval boundaries;
- SQLite one-worker limit;
- staging persistence status;
- field-integration status;
- claims that remain unproven.
