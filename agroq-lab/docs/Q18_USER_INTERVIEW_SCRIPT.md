# AgroQ Q18 User Interview Script

## Opening

AgroQ is in controlled beta. The purpose of this interview is to understand current manual work, reporting delays, evidence requirements, infrastructure limits, and risks. This interview is not a sales promise and does not authorize automatic field actions.

## Core questions

1. What work are you doing today?
2. Which parts are paper-based, spreadsheet-based, or dependent on memory?
3. Where do delays, duplicate entry, or missing evidence occur?
4. Who records information, who reviews it, and who approves action?
5. Which data already exists?
6. Which devices, networks, computers, and storage are available?
7. What must continue working without internet access?
8. Which actions must always remain human-approved?
9. What would make a small pilot useful?
10. What must be excluded from the first pilot?
11. What evidence would demonstrate value?
12. What risks or privacy concerns would block participation?

## Record

- Goals
- Pain points
- Current workflow
- Success criteria
- Risk notes
- Follow-up decision

## Decision options

- Continue discovery
- Pilot candidate
- Follow up later
- Not a fit
