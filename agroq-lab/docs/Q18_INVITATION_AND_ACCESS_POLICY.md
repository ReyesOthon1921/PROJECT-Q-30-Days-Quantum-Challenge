# AgroQ Q18 Invitation and Access Policy

## Default invitation controls

- Administrator-only invitation creation
- No public administrator invitation
- Default expiration: 14 days
- Default uses: 1
- Absolute maximum uses: 5
- Allowed roles: viewer, researcher, field operator
- Email binding recommended
- Audit event required
- Automatic deactivation after final allowed use

## Access-request review

Statuses:

- new
- reviewing
- approved
- declined
- archived

Approval does not automatically create an account. The administrator creates a controlled invitation after reviewing role, purpose, consent, and scope.

## Beta reservations

Statuses:

- interest recorded
- contacted
- approved
- declined
- archived

Reservation records interest only. They do not promise access, pricing, deployment, or participation.
