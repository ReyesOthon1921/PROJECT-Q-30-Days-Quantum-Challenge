# AgroQ Q19 Claims Register Policy

Every public, investor, research, agricultural, security, operational, and quantum statement must be linked to an evidence level and limitations.

## Evidence levels

1. Idea
2. Prototype
3. Simulation
4. Controlled beta
5. Field verified
6. Publication

## Statuses

- Draft
- Approved
- Restricted
- Rejected
- Retired

## Automatic restrictions

Quantum, agricultural, and operational claims at idea, prototype, or simulation level cannot be approved as established performance claims.

## Required limitations

Every claim records:

- what was tested;
- what data was used;
- whether evidence was synthetic, simulated, controlled-beta, field, or published;
- hardware status;
- operational dependency status;
- known limitations;
- evidence reference;
- reviewer.
