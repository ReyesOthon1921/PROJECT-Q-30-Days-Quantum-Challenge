## Q24 — AI, media, synthetic data, and research boundaries

AgroQ may use AI-assisted code, writing, analysis, and concept media. Public
concept media must be labeled when a reasonable viewer could mistake it for
real field evidence or an operating deployment. Human review remains required
before publication.

The investor prototype uses synthetic demonstration data. It does not show live
field measurements, validated agricultural performance, autonomous field
control, or proven quantum advantage.

## Q25 — California-facing operating safeguards

This implementation uses conservative disclosure controls. It does not state
that every listed statute necessarily applies to AgroQ in every circumstance.
Applicability depends on facts including business status, audience, revenue,
data practices, product design, and transaction terms.

## Preliminary Founding Grower terms

- First 100 approved operations: proposed $500 first-year rate.
- Approved operations 101–200: proposed $800 first-year rate.
- After the first 200: planned $1,000 first-year rate.
- Possible year-two annual rate: planned $1,500.
- Applications are non-binding and do not reserve a position.
- No payment is collected through the public application.
- No automatic renewal is currently offered.
- Final eligibility, acceptance, pricing, scope, benefits, and availability
  require written confirmation.
- Hardware, sensors, laboratory services, shipping, travel, installation,
  training, integrations, and on-site support require a separate written scope.

## Review boundary

These controls are product documentation, not legal advice. Before accepting
payment or activating a paid subscription, AgroQ should have California counsel
review the final customer agreement, privacy notice, renewal flow, media
permissions, and actual data-sharing practices.
