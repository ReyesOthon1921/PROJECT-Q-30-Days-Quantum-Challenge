# AgroQ MVP Master Build Brief

## Product statement

AgroQ Acre MVP is a mobile-friendly, manual-first field operations and research system for a one-acre living laboratory. It records the physical configuration, human work, observations, experiments, recommendations, approvals, and measured outcomes.

## Primary user

The initial user is the founder/research orchestrator working with field technicians, students, agronomy or biology advisors, and engineering collaborators.

## Problem being solved

Agricultural R&D becomes fragmented when measurements live in notebooks, sensor platforms use separate dashboards, physical repairs are undocumented, model outputs lack provenance, and decisions cannot be traced to outcomes.

## MVP promise

A team can run one complete research cycle even without connected infrastructure:

1. Register a plot, asset, or experiment.
2. Create and assign manual work.
3. Record a measurement in the field.
4. Continue recording while offline.
5. Review data quality and context.
6. Produce a recommendation from a transparent rule or model.
7. Require a documented human decision.
8. Record the resulting action and outcome.
9. Export the evidence package.

## Non-goals

The first MVP will not:

- autonomously operate pumps, valves, vehicles, or biological-release equipment;
- diagnose crop disease as medical or regulatory certainty;
- claim quantum advantage;
- require an acre-wide sensor deployment;
- require a cloud warehouse;
- replace laboratory testing or expert review.

## Success demonstration

A five-minute demonstration should show:

1. Dashboard and acre registry.
2. Manual soil-moisture entry.
3. Offline entry saved locally.
4. Work task completed.
5. Recommendation reviewed and approved or rejected.
6. Research records exported.
7. Sensor and model adapters shown as the next expansion point.
