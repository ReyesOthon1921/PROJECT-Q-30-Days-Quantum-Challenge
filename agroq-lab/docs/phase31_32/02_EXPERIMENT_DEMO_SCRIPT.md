# Three-Experiment Demonstration Script

## Experiment 1 — Low-Water Biomass Retention

Show six synthetic treatment plants, six synthetic controls, fresh shoot biomass, canopy area, leaf count, wilting, moisture, human review, and export.

## Experiment 2 — Heat Resilience / Bolting Delay

Show the synthetic warm-condition group, baseline control, bolting-delay score, supporting biomass observations, and the administrator approval decision.

## Experiment 3 — Sequence-to-Pigmentation Evidence Map

Show push-button NCBI DNA or protein search, one-click insertion, accession provenance, sequence checksum, experiment linking, human interpretation, and FASTA/JSON/CSV export.

## Required truth statement

These are software and evidence-workflow demonstrations. They do not prove a new variety, field performance, or genetic causality.
