# Manual Operations Playbook

Manual work is not a temporary workaround. It is the reference process used to validate future automation.

## Morning field round

1. Confirm gateway or paper backup availability.
2. Review open work tasks and pending approvals.
3. Walk each active experimental block.
4. Record visible conditions and unusual events.
5. Take scheduled manual measurements.
6. Photograph or tag anything requiring later review.
7. Check sensor enclosures and power condition.
8. Synchronize queued entries.
9. Escalate safety, biological, or equipment concerns to the responsible reviewer.

## Manual observation minimum

Every record should include:

- plot;
- date and time;
- observed property;
- value and unit;
- instrument or method;
- operator;
- quality flag;
- relevant conditions;
- notes or evidence reference.

## Manual sampling minimum

Each physical sample should receive:

```text
Sample ID
Plot and local position
Depth or plant location
Collection time
Collector
Method
Container
Storage condition
Requested analysis
Transfer history
Laboratory result
```

## Calibration

1. Identify the instrument by asset ID.
2. Record the reference standard and method.
3. Capture before-adjustment results.
4. Capture correction or coefficient.
5. Capture after-adjustment results.
6. Mark the effective date and responsible person.
7. Do not overwrite measurements already collected under the older calibration.

## Repair

1. Create a fault task.
2. Record symptoms and environmental conditions.
3. Isolate the affected component.
4. Replace only the failed module where possible.
5. Record removed and installed part numbers.
6. Perform a defined return-to-service test.
7. Update revision and status.
8. Preserve the failed component for analysis when useful.

## Human approval

High-impact recommendations remain pending until a reviewer records:

- decision;
- evidence considered;
- edits;
- constraints;
- responsible person;
- decision time;
- required follow-up measurement.
