INTERPRETATION
The ideal channel should produce matching sifted keys and approximately 0% QBER.
An intercept-and-resend attack should produce an average QBER near 25% because Eve guesses the wrong basis approximately half the time, and half of those disturbed states produce incorrect bits.
A QBER above the educational detection threshold causes the session to be rejected.

NOTE
