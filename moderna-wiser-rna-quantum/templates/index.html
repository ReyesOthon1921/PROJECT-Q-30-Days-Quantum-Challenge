Research Context

Therapeutic Motivation + QUBO Limits

Updated Context

RNA Therapeutics Context

RNA therapeutics and mRNA vaccines make RNA sequence design, stability, translation, and structure prediction important research problems. This dashboard uses RNA secondary-structure prediction as the computational bridge between biological motivation and optimization modeling.

Quantum Annealer / QUBO Limitation

Recent RNA/QUBO work warns that QUBO-based RNA folding can struggle as sequence length grows because of deep local minima, two-body interaction limits, overprediction of stems/base pairs, and missing higher-order RNA constraints.

Research Position

This project does not claim quantum advantage. It compares classical benchmarks, QUBO baselines, simulated annealing, QAOA readiness, and VQE readiness as a transparent research prototype.
