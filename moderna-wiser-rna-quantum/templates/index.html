Paper-Informed Research Note

QUBO Scaling Limitations

New Paper Added

After reviewing the paper on modern quantum annealers and RNA structure prediction, this dashboard now includes scaling plots to make the limitations visible. The paper argues that QUBO-based RNA folding can struggle as sequence length grows because of deep local minima, two-body interaction limits, overprediction of stems/base pairs, and missing higher-order RNA structural constraints.

This project does not claim quantum advantage. The goal is to build a transparent research prototype that shows both the potential and the limits of QUBO, simulated annealing, QAOA readiness, and VQE readiness for RNA/mRNA secondary-structure prediction.
